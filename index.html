ABOUT US

About Camalia Enterprise Ltd

Camalia Enterprise Ltd is a Kenyan landscaping and garden services company dedicated to creating beautiful, functional, and sustainable outdoor spaces. With years of experience in landscaping, construction, horticulture, and garden maintenance, we provide professional solutions for residential, commercial, institutional, and property development projects.

What We Do

At Camalia Enterprise Ltd, we offer a complete range of landscaping and outdoor services, including:

🎨 Landscape Design & Architecture

Professional planning and design of beautiful outdoor spaces tailored to each property.

🏗️ Landscaping Construction

Complete installation and development of gardens, lawns, pathways, outdoor features, and landscape structures.

🧱 Hardscaping

Cabro paving, walkways, patios, retaining walls, garden structures, and other hard landscape features.

🌿 Softscaping

Supply and planting of trees, palms, flowers, shrubs, hedges, lawns, and other ornamental plants.

✂️ Garden & Landscape Maintenance

Regular lawn care, pruning, trimming, planting, fertilizing, cleaning, and general garden maintenance.

🏊 Swimming Pool Construction

Swimming pool construction and surrounding landscape development.

🌸 Plant Nursery & Florist Services

Supply of ornamental plants, palms, flowers, indoor plants, and other landscaping materials.

🛣️ Camalia Cabro Roll Installation

Professional installation of cabro paving for driveways, pathways, parking areas, compounds, and other outdoor spaces.

Our Approach

We combine creative landscape design, quality workmanship, and reliable maintenance to transform ordinary spaces into attractive and enjoyable environments. Whether you need a complete landscape from the ground up or ongoing garden maintenance, our team works closely with clients to deliver practical solutions that suit their property, style, and budget.
